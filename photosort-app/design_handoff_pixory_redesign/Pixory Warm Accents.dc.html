Coral — flat, simplest
A single solid hue. Calmer and easier to keep consistent than a gradient, still warm and friendly.
On combos
For a single-accent app I'd keep it to one warm hue (or one tight gradient) and let the photos provide every other color — that's what makes a gallery UI feel premium.
If you want a pairing
Coral + deep teal — warm CTA, cool calm surfaces
Amber + plum-black base — warmer dark, richer
Coral + honey — two warms, secondary highlights
